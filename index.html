Interactive Math Simulations

College algebra simulations and problem generators built for adult, military-affiliated learners. Each one asks the learner to predict, build, and test before the math gives anything away.

Simulations

Quadratic functions · Interactive textbook

Quadratic Equations: An Interactive Field Guide

A page-turning flipbook with 18 pages and 9 hands-on activities: aim a hose stream at a window, hunt zeros, place a vertex with the mirror test, and finish with a mission check. Progress fills in on the back cover.

Learners build the full quadratic toolkit one feature at a time. Circles · Distance & midpoint

Bravo Two-Seven: Where Does the Center Go?

A pilot ejects somewhere on a flight leg. Fuel allows one search circle. Place the center, launch the helicopters, and find out whether your math found the pilot.

Learners discover why the endpoints of a diameter fix the center. Pythagorean theorem · Right triangles

Ladder Rescue Simulator

Set a building height and base distance and watch the ladder and climb angle respond. Then work six emergency rescues and decide whether the truck ladder reaches.

Learners connect c = √(a² + b²) to a safety decision with real stakes. Quadratic functions

Water Balloon Launch

Tune angle, speed, and platform height to hit a target, then read a parabola's intercepts and vertex to write its equation and test it with a launch.

Learners connect roots, vertex, and coefficients to a real trajectory. Quadratic functions

Interactive Quadratic Explorer

Slide a, b, and c and watch the vertex, intercepts, axis of symmetry, and discriminant update as the grid rescales to fit.

Learners see what each coefficient controls before they memorize it. Pythagorean theorem

Pythagorean Squares

Build squares on each leg of a right triangle, count their areas, and predict the hypotenuse before the answer appears.

Learners see a² + b² = c² as a statement about area. Circles · Distance formula

Travel Limit Grapher

Learners graph their own answers to a travel-limit circle problem. The tool flags a missing right angle or a circle that misses its building.

Learners check their own work against the geometry instead of an answer key. Complex numbers

Modulus as Distance

Drag a complex number and watch a right triangle build |z| from its real and imaginary parts, then extend the idea to the distance between two points.

Learners see |a + bi| as the Pythagorean theorem in disguise. Complex numbers

Complex Plane Arithmetic

Add complex numbers as vectors, then multiply them and watch the product rotate and stretch. Try z₂ = i, z₂ = −1, and the conjugate.

Learners see multiplication as rotation plus scaling.

Personalized problem generators

Each learner gets unique numbers and a story built from a seed, so work can't be copied but every version has the same structure.

Linear · Quadratic · Exponential

Story Problem Generator

Every learner gets a unique story with a linear, a quadratic, and an exponential model, plus a code that brings the same version back. Learners plot points on built-in grids and export a PDF.

One story, three function families, compared side by side. Quadratic functions

Build Your Own Quadratic Story

Learners pick a genre and a character and get a personalized four-part problem: test a shot, fix it, find a hidden launcher, and build a parabola from one point.

Seeded versions give every learner different numbers with the same structure. Exponential & logistic growth

Network Infection Mission Brief

A callsign generates a cyber intrusion mission. Learners model the breach with exponential and logistic growth, find the tipping point, and brief the commander. Exports to Word.

Learners compare unlimited growth with growth that hits a capacity.
