Try This:
  • Set b = 0 and c = 0. Slide a from negative to positive. What happens to the direction the graph opens? What happens to the width as |a| gets bigger or smaller?
  • Keep a and c fixed. Slide b back and forth. What happens to the vertex? Does the y-intercept ever change?
  • Keep a and b fixed. Slide c up and down. What part of the graph moves, and what stays the same?
  • Set a = 0. What kind of function do you get now? What do b and c represent in this case?
  • Can you find values of a, b, and c that give a graph with no x-intercepts? What does the discriminant look like in that case?
  • Try a small |a| (like -0.05) with a larger b and c, similar to the equations in your story problems. Notice how the grid zooms out automatically.
