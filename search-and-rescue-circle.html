Bravo Two-Seven: Where Does the Center Go?

A search circle, a fuel limit, and one point that decides everything.

0347, North Pacific. A friendly radar track blinks out. No mayday. Nineteen minutes later the pilot's beacon fires four weak pulses, then goes silent.

The chart shows two points: last radar contact and the final beacon fix. They are the endpoints of the flight leg, the circle's diagonal. The pilot ejected somewhere on that diagonal. Nobody knows where.

Fuel allows one search circle with a radius of half the diagonal. Lieutenant Aldana wants the center at last radar contact: "It's the only hard data we have." Lieutenant Reyes disagrees. You build the plan.
